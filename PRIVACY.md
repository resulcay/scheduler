# Scheduler – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

This policy covers the **Scheduler: Events & Reminders** app (Android and iOS,
bundle ID `com.resoftware.scheduler`). [Türkçe aşağıda.](#türkçe)

## Summary

Scheduler does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything
you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

- **Your events** – title, description, date, time, color and reminder setting.
  They are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not
  uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app.
- **Your settings** – language, light/dark mode and list style, stored the same way.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally through your device's operating system
(Android alarms / iOS local notifications). No push server is involved and
nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn notifications off at any time in
your device settings.

## Internet and other apps

The app works fully offline. It only leaves the app when you ask it to:

- **Rate** opens the app's page in the App Store or Google Play.
- **Share** opens your device's share sheet with a link to the app. What you send
  and to whom is up to you.
- **Add to clock** (Android only, optional) creates an alarm in your device's
  clock app.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs
- No tracking, no device identifiers, no location access
- No data sale or sharing with third parties

## Children

The app collects no data and is suitable for all ages.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with
a new "Last updated" date.
