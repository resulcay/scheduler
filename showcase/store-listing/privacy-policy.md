# Scheduler - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-03_

Scheduler does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

- **Your events** (titles, descriptions, dates, colors) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. Nothing is sent over the internet.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking** are used.
- **Internet access** is only used when you choose to open the store page (Rate) or share the app link.
- **System alarm** (Android only, optional) is created in your device's clock app when you ask for it.

Children: the app contains no data collection and is suitable for all ages.
