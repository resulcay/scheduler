## en-US

**App name** (30): Scheduler - Events & Reminders

**Short description** (80):
Plan events in seconds and get reminders right on time, every time.

**Full description**:
Scheduler keeps your appointments, meetings, trips and deadlines in one colorful place and reminds you right on time.

• Create an event in seconds: title, description, date, time and color
• Reminders when the event starts, plus an optional heads-up 1 hour to 1 year before
• Hourly countdown reminders for the last 3 or 6 hours
• Reminders survive phone restarts and app updates
• Optional system alarm for events within the next 24 hours
• Stacked cards or classic list view
• Light and dark mode
• English and Turkish
• No account, no ads, no tracking - your events never leave your phone

**What's new (1.1.0)**:
Notifications are fixed! Reminders now arrive on time, also after a restart. New: a notification at the event time, hourly countdowns, a refreshed event screen and many stability fixes.
