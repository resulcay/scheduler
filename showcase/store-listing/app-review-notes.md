2. Purpose and audience
Scheduler is a simple offline planner for anyone who wants to remember appointments, meetings, trips and deadlines. The user creates an event (title, description, date, time, color) and the app reminds them with a local notification when it starts, optionally also 1 hour to 1 year before, or hourly during the last 3 or 6 hours. It solves forgetting important events without needing an account, a calendar service or an internet connection.

3. How to use it
No login, credentials or sample files are needed.
- On first launch, allow notifications when asked.
- Tap "+" to create an event: enter a title, tap "Pick" to choose a date and time a few minutes ahead, keep "Notify Me" on, optionally choose an advance reminder, tap "Save Event".
- The notification arrives at the chosen time (also when the app is closed).
- Tap an event to see its details; the trash icon deletes it.
- The menu at the top left switches language (English/Turkish), dark mode and list view, and offers Share and Rate.

4. External services
None. The app works fully offline and stores events only on the device. Reminders use iOS local notifications (UserNotifications). There is no backend, analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI service. "Rate" opens the App Store page, and "Share" opens the system share sheet with the App Store link.

5. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is available in English and Turkish.
